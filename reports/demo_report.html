Equity Factor
Research Lab

A standalone demonstration of factor construction, cross-sectional processing, chronological model selection and cost-aware portfolio evaluation.

Synthetic demonstration only. These results were generated by the included simulator. They do not represent the original research archive, real securities, employer results or evidence of investable alpha.

Experiment

Seed 42; 260 business dates; 60 fictional securities; eight features; twelve sampled intraday observations per security per day. Business dates are not an exchange holiday calendar.

Signal: after close t. Execution: open to close t+1. Dates split approximately 60% / 20% / 20%; labels crossing a partition boundary are purged. Ridge alpha is chosen on validation rank IC. The scaler and selected model retain their training-only fit.

What a reviewer can verify

CSV files contain raw factor exposures, processed exposures, test predictions, model coefficients, validation scores, daily portfolio returns and positions. The JSON summary records input settings, package versions, date partitions and selected alpha.

Research limits

Latent return structure is deliberately built into the simulator. The short holdout cannot establish statistical significance. Cross-sectional residualization precedes rank transformation, so final ranked features need not remain exactly industry or size neutral. The portfolio is a research abstraction: no borrow availability, financing, exchange price limits, execution delay, queueing or capacity model is included. A-share T+1 rules and short-sale constraints can make this intraday long/short portfolio infeasible.
